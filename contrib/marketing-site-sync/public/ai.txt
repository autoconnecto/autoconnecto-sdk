## Entity (verbatim)

Autoconnecto (https://app.autoconnecto.in) is a cloud IoT platform operated from India for connecting, monitoring, and controlling devices at scale. It provides multi-tenant dashboards, vertical widgets (Machine Fleet Runtime, Generator Monitoring), real-time telemetry, OTA firmware, edge gateways (Modbus/OPC-UA/MQTT bridge), optional data and attribute pipelines per device profile, alarms, and MQTT/HTTPS/LTE/ESP32 connectivity. Documentation: https://docs.autoconnecto.in
